Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SolitaireDeck ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application Solitaire Deck (the "App"). By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Data stored on your device:

  • Game progress, scores, and statistics
  • Settings and preferences (theme, audio, language)
  • App configuration data

Automatically collected technical data:

  • App events (game start, end, win/loss, moves, hints used)
  • Session length and frequency
  • Device type, OS version, language, and region
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), subject to your consent

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve game features and stability
  • Diagnose and fix crashes and bugs
  • Analyze gameplay to improve balance
  • Deliver relevant advertising
  • Measure advertising effectiveness and install attribution
  • Comply with legal obligations

On iOS, we may request permission to access your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before using it for personalized advertising. You can change this permission at any time in: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. EU/EEA Users — GDPR

If you are located in the EU, EEA, or UK, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. A consent screen is shown on first launch in EU/EEA countries. To exercise your rights, contact: support@SolitaireDeck.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. California Residents — CCPA

California residents may request to know, delete, or opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. To submit a request: support@SolitaireDeck.com.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Contact us at support@SolitaireDeck.com to request deletion of any such data.

9. Data Retention

  • Local device data: retained until you uninstall the App
  • Analytics data: per each provider's retention schedule
  • Crash logs: up to 90 days

10. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your residence, including the United States, in accordance with applicable law.
